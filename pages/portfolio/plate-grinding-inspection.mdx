    <li style={{ marginBottom: '1rem' }}><strong>Closed-Loop Autonomous Pipeline:</strong> Helped deliver a fully autonomous scan-detect-checkpoint-grind-inspect pipeline requiring no manual operator intervention between cycles.</li>
    <li style={{ marginBottom: '1rem' }}><strong>Dimensional Accuracy:</strong> Achieved grinding depth control to within measurement uncertainty of ±0.005", with zero over-grinding events across an eight-pass validation campaign and a 34% improvement in post-grind surface uniformity.</li>
