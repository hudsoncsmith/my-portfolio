# 📚 Reading List

I am an avid reader and enjoy all forms of print media. I subscribe to *The New York Times* and *The Wall Street Journal*, and I love books across multiple genres—including novels, science fiction, nonfiction, self-help, and philosophy. 

I often share my book recommendations with family, friends, and colleagues. This list includes some of my all-time favorite books alongside some recent reads I've been enjoying.

---

## 🧠 Nonfiction
<BookList>
- *Regenesis: Feeding the World Without Devouring the Planet* — George Monbiot
- *Guns, Germs and Steel: The fates of Human Societies* - Jared Diamond
- *Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!* - Richard P. Feynman
- *Hitching Rides with Buddha* - Will Ferguson
- *The Chrysanthemum and the Sword* - Ruth Benedict
- *A Brief History of Time* - Stephen Hawking
- *Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind* - Yuval Noah Harari
- *A Short History of Nearly Everything* - Bill Bryson
</BookList>

<Callout>
*Regensis* highlights the lack of robustness in the current food system and the challenges that monoculture crops and current livestock farming methods could face in the coming years.
</Callout>
