<div style={{ maxWidth: '1000px', margin: '0 auto', padding: '0 2rem 4rem' }}>
  <h2 style={{ fontSize: '1.8rem', color: '#0056b3', borderBottom: '2px solid #e0e0e0', paddingBottom: '0.5rem', marginBottom: '1.5rem' }}>Project Overview</h2>
  <p style={{ fontSize: '1.1rem', lineHeight: '1.8', marginBottom: '2rem', color: '#333' }}>
    As technical lead, I directed process development for this commercial R&D NRE program, integrating a robotic belt sander into our Scan&Grind platform to automate the removal of large welds on curved substrates. The work established a goal-surface characterization method for the robot to plan against, a set of process physics governing safe and efficient material removal, and the toolpath directionality and reachability logic needed to translate those constraints into a working grinding path.
  </p>

  <h2 style={{ fontSize: '1.8rem', color: '#0056b3', borderBottom: '2px solid #e0e0e0', paddingBottom: '0.5rem', marginBottom: '1.5rem' }}>Key Contributions</h2>
  <ul style={{ fontSize: '1.1rem', lineHeight: '1.8', marginBottom: '2rem', color: '#333', paddingLeft: '1.5rem' }}>
    <li style={{ marginBottom: '1rem' }}><strong>Goal Surface Characterization:</strong> Developed a method to isolate weld features from a scanned point cloud using color detection and fit a cylindrical goal surface to the remaining part geometry, giving the robot a reference and stop point for successive grinding passes.</li>
    <li style={{ marginBottom: '1rem' }}><strong>Toolpath Directionality:</strong> Established a radial grinding direction so the material removal profile stayed consistent across the full width of the belt, after initial tangent-direction passes showed uneven removal.</li>
    <li style={{ marginBottom: '1rem' }}><strong>Reachability-Driven Path Offsets:</strong> Built logic to offset the toolpath laterally as material was removed, keeping the belt sander's housing clear of the part as the tool progressed deeper into the weld.</li>
    <li style={{ marginBottom: '1rem' }}><strong>Process Physics & Removal Rate Characterization:</strong> Ran structured grinding experiments across force, RPM, and abrasive grit combinations to characterize material removal rate, heat buildup, and consumable wear, converging on a validated process recipe.</li>
    <li style={{ marginBottom: '1rem' }}><strong>Toolpath Planner Preconditions:</strong> Defined conservative per-pass and per-two-pass material removal limits, derived from the measured removal rate, as preconditions ensuring the planner always converges on the goal surface without overshoot.</li>
  </ul>
